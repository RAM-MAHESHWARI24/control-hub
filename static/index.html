      Add an Android TV

      Connect a TV over Wi-Fi/Ethernet ADB in 5 phone-friendly steps.

      Step 1 of 5 Enable Developer Options

      1. Turn on Developer Options

      Open Settings → System → About and select Android TV OS build 7 times until “You are now a developer!” appears.

      2. Turn on USB & Network Debugging

      Return to Settings, open Developer options, and turn on USB debugging and Network debugging (ADB over network).

      3. Find your TV’s IP Address

      Check Settings → Network & Internet on your TV, or scan the local network for open ADB ports:

      Tip: Set a static IP (DHCP reservation) for this TV in your router so power outages never change its address and interrupt updates.

      4. Pair & Authorize on the TV Screen

      Tap Connect to TV below. On first connection, look at your TV screen and choose Always allow from this computer.

      5. Name Your TV & Pick Managed Apps

      Detected TV metadata will appear here.
